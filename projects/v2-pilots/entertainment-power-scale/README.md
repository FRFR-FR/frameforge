# Entertainment pilot — Solar Sentinel vs Rift Warden

**FrameForge V2 pilot · 12 seconds · 1280×720 · 24 FPS**

A fictional matchup between two original character designs. No franchise names, artwork, likenesses, logos, screenshots or footage are used. All four SVG assets are procedural and rights-reviewed.

## Visual treatment
- Character cut-out reveals with animated movement and energy halos.
- Floating sparks, a pulse-ring transition and symmetrical matchup layout.
- Side-by-side illustrative profile bars for power, mobility and durability.
- A scenario-dependent closing card defining arena, goal and win conditions.

The profile bars are **illustrative demo values**, not canonical statistics, research findings or scientific measurements. They must not be presented as factual evidence.

## Contents and review
- project.json — editable V2 project with six separate timeline tracks.
- media_manifest.json — provenance and rights record for each SVG.
- assets/ — original editable vector characters, background and comparison layout.
- narration.txt — timed voiceover draft.
- The pilot workflow creates pilot.mp4, original-sfx.wav, QA output, contact sheet/review page and a delivery ZIP.

The video contains original synthesized transition/interface SFX only. No TTS voice or third-party soundtrack is bundled. Record a voiceover and re-mix it before publication. Human review is required; automated QA is technical, not subjective approval.
