# Underneath Everything — Cinematic Technology Documentaries

This folder contains the launch blueprint for a growth-first, cinematic YouTube documentary channel. The working identity is **Underneath Everything**; the final public name and handle remain subject to availability checks.

## Channel promise
Reveal the hidden systems, machines and engineering that make modern life possible. Every episode should make a complex subject understandable through original storytelling, credible research, real-world footage and clear visual explanations.

## Launch focus
**Hidden infrastructure**, beginning with submarine telecommunications cables.

## Production targets
- Long-form: 8–12 minutes, 1920×1080, 30 fps.
- Shorts: 20–45 seconds, cut as distinct mini-stories from long-form research and footage.
- Initial cadence: 1–2 polished long-form videos per week, then move toward 3 only after the pipeline consistently meets quality gates.
- No publishing quota overrides accuracy, rights clearance, or visual quality.

## Production principles
1. Research first; script only from verified claims.
2. Use licensed/permissioned footage and retain source/license records.
3. Use original diagrams, maps and 3D cutaways to explain what footage cannot show.
4. AI may assist research, narration and editing, but the final documentary must contain meaningful human editorial decisions and original value.
5. Never fabricate footage, quotes, interviews, statistics or technical incidents.
6. Review the rendered video and audio before publishing.
