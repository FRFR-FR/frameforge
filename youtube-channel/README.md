# Underneath Everything — Cinematic Technology Documentaries

This folder contains the launch blueprint and production system for a growth-first, cinematic YouTube documentary channel. The working identity is **Underneath Everything**; the public handle and trademark remain subject to availability checks.

## Channel promise
Reveal the hidden systems, machines and engineering that make modern life possible. Every episode should make a complex subject understandable through original storytelling, credible research, rights-cleared footage and clear visual explanations.

## Launch focus
**Hidden infrastructure**, beginning with submarine telecommunications cables.

## Strict export lock
- Long-form master: **1280×720 (720p), 16:9, MP4 H.264 + AAC**.
- Shorts: **720×1280**, 9:16.
- Never export or deliver 1080p or 4K.
- Target 24–30 fps only when the actual render can complete reliably; always report the measured frame rate and never fake it.
- Runtime target: 8–12 minutes when the story and narration support it; never stretch scenes to hit a quota.

## Production principles
1. Research first; script only from verified claims.
2. Use licensed/permissioned footage and retain source/license records.
3. Use original diagrams, maps and 3D cutaways to explain what footage cannot show.
4. AI may assist research, narration and editing, but every episode must have original editorial value.
5. Never fabricate footage, quotes, interviews, statistics or technical incidents.
6. Review the rendered video and audio before publication.
7. No automatic YouTube publishing; a human must approve every upload.
