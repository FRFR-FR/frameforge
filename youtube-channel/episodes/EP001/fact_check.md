# EP001 — Fact-check and editorial caveats

Research checked: 26 November 2026. Figures are dated snapshots, not live counters.

| Claim ID | Claim used in narration | Verification | Editorial caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | Submarine cables carry more than 99% of international data flows. | ITU Global Connectivity Report 2025, Chapter 3. | Say “international data flows”; do not claim every internet packet or local connection uses a submarine cable. |
| C02 | ITU's 2025 snapshot listed 500+ operational systems and about 1.4 million km. | ITU Global Connectivity Report 2025, Chapter 3. | Treat as the report's 2025 snapshot, not a live count. |
| C03 | ICPC's May 2026 FAQ describes about 500 systems and around 1.8 million km. | ICPC Media Enquiries & FAQs, updated 20 May 2026. | ICPC's current overview and ITU's 2025 snapshot use different dates/scope; do not combine the totals. |
| C04 | ICPC says about 150–200 faults occur globally each year, with 70–80% caused by accidental human activity. | ICPC Media Enquiries & FAQs, updated 20 May 2026. | Attribute the range to ICPC. The ITU 2025 report separately states 86% of faults result from human activity; the two sources differ. Do not average them or imply a single universally agreed rate. |
| C05 | Route surveys consider depth, seabed morphology, existing cables/pipes, fishing/mining activity, currents, seismic activity and legal constraints. | ITU-T G.971 (12/2024), Annex A.3.1. | Generalized description only; do not show exact sensitive routes or operational vulnerabilities. |
| C06 | Cable ships control payout; burial may use a towed sea plough or submersible equipment. | ITU-T G.971 (12/2024), Annex A.3.2 and Appendix I. | Methods vary by water depth, seabed and system design. |
| C07 | Cable construction varies by protection category and environment. | ITU-T G.978 (05/2025), clauses 5.1 and 5.3.4. | Avoid presenting one cable cross-section as universal. Label the graphic “simplified / illustrative”. |
| C08 | Submarine repeaters/amplifiers are housed and powered as part of the system. | ITU-T G.971 (12/2024) and ITU-T G. Suppl. 41 (07/2024). | Specific repeater architecture varies; avoid implying every system has identical equipment. |

## Important source disagreement
The ITU's 2025 Global Connectivity Report says 86% of faults result from human activities. The ICPC FAQ updated 20 May 2026 says 70–80% are caused by accidental human activity. The narration uses the ICPC range and names ICPC. A future revision may explain the differing scopes/methodologies in a lower-third or narration note.

## Exclusions
- No claim that a single cable break will disconnect the whole world.
- No claim that any single chokepoint carries a large share of all global internet traffic.
- No speculative attribution of faults to sabotage.
- No precise cable route coordinates or sensitive vulnerability details.
