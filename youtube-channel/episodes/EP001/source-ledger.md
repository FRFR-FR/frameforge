## Discrepancy handling

The ITU 2025 chapter reports 86% of faults from human activities, while the ICPC's May 2026 FAQ reports 70–80% accidental human activity. These figures have different source methods and scopes; the narration uses the ICPC's 70–80% estimate and should attribute it explicitly. Do not combine the percentages or present them as a single definitive measurement.

## Unsupported / avoid

- “Every internet connection travels through an undersea cable.” False overstatement.
- “One cable break disconnects a continent.” False overstatement; traffic may reroute, though capacity and latency can be affected.
- “Most cable damage is sabotage.” Not supported; ICPC says accidental activity is predominant.
- Precise operational cable routes or vulnerabilities without a clear public-interest reason and authoritative public source.
- Using the commercial TeleGeography map as a video asset without permission.
