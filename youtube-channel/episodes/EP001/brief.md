## Editorial angle

The internet appears wireless at the user end, but international connectivity relies heavily on physical infrastructure. Follow a data message from a device to terrestrial networks, across a submarine cable, through landing stations, and onward to its destination. The story should demystify the system without turning ordinary maintenance faults into a sabotage thriller.

## Three title candidates

1. The Secret Cables That Keep the Internet Alive
2. The Hidden Cables Carrying the World's Internet
3. The Underwater Network Connecting the World

Avoid saying that every internet packet travels underwater. The statistic used in the narration refers to international data flows.
