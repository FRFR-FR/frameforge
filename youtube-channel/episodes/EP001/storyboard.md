## Production note

The delivered pilot uses original vector/procedural motion graphics throughout. No third-party footage is used or represented as real footage. The visual system is coherent but not yet at the requested maximum cinematic quality; the next pass should use licensed live-action footage and more sophisticated 3D cutaways rather than adding decorative effects.
