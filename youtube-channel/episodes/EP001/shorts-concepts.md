1. **The internet has a physical floor** (25–35 seconds): hook — “Your message may cross an ocean without ever touching a satellite.” Explain that submarine fibre cables carry the vast majority of international data flows. Payoff: the internet has a physical seabed layer.
2. **What happens when an undersea cable breaks?** (30–45 seconds): hook — “A cable fault does not automatically disconnect a country.” Explain monitoring, alternative routes and repair ships; note that impact depends on route diversity and capacity.
3. **Why are some submarine cables armoured?** (20–30 seconds): hook — “The same cable does not need the same protection everywhere.” Compare shallow-water protection with lighter deep-water cable types, using ITU-T G.978.
