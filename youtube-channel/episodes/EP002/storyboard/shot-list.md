## Editing constraints
- Master is 1280×720, 16:9, MP4 H.264/AAC only.
- Absolutely no captions, subtitles, SRT/VTT, text overlays, labels, title cards, lower thirds or logo bug.
- No generic circuit-board loops, irrelevant stock or static slide sequences.
- At least 70% relevant moving real-world footage; aim 75–85%.
- Original 3D scenes must be documented as reconstructions, not camera footage.
- Use real measured narration length. Do not stretch shots or create filler to force 8–12 minutes.
