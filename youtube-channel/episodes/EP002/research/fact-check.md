# Fact-check — EP002

Research cut-off: 26 November 2026. Manufacturer specifications are attributed, and the script avoids treating process-node labels as literal dimensions.

| Claim | Source | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| EUV wavelength is 13.5 nm. | S01–S03 | Not a transistor width or node name. |
| Tin droplets are about 25 μm, travel around 70 m/s, are hit by two laser pulses and processed up to 50,000 times per second. | S02 | ASML source description. |
| EUV is absorbed by air and ordinary glass; mirrors and a high-vacuum path are used. | S01, S03 | Specially engineered multilayer mirrors are used. |
| ZEISS describes Mo/Si multilayer mirror reflectivity up to about 70%. | S03 | Upper reflectivity value, not total system throughput. |
| ASML describes 4x reduction of the reticle pattern. | S01 | Attribute to ASML. |
| ASML describes quarter-nanometre wafer-stage positioning and 20,000 checks/corrections per second. | S01 | Product specification, not a tolerance for every fab component. |
| Chips can include up to 100 layers and hundreds of steps; EUV/DUV both remain in use. | S04 | Upper-end summary; actual stack varies. |
| EXE:5000 uses 0.55 NA, has an 8 nm resolution specification and vendor comparisons of 1.7x smaller features/2.9x density. | S06 | Vendor claims, not guaranteed product outcomes or node naming. |
| High-NA EUV reached HVM for selected layers in a subset of Intel products. | S07 | Scope is limited; do not imply all products/layers. |
| The million-wafer milestone aggregates certification/testing, R&D and selected production. | S08 | Not one million finished chips or production-only wafers. |
| Pixabay content may be adapted into creative works but not redistributed standalone. | S10, S11 | Verify each item for third-party marks, people, property and other rights. |

## Required wording
- “Two-nanometre” and “three-nanometre” are generation labels, not a claim that every feature measures that width.
- One EUV scanner does not manufacture an entire processor.
- Not every layer is EUV-printed.
- High-NA's million-wafer metric includes several stages, not only volume production.
- Conceptual 3D cutaways are not real recordings of proprietary machinery.
- Generic laser or cleanroom stock is context, not evidence of a specific ASML/Intel operation.
