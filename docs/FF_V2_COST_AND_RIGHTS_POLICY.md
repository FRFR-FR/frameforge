

## 8. Imported VFX-first priority

For production VFX, prefer externally authored effects with an asset-specific, checked free-use licence over equivalent repository-authored procedural graphics. Keep the source binary in the temporary render workspace, not the Git repository, unless redistribution is explicitly permitted and storage is justified. Preserve the creator, item page, licence, intended use, restrictions and attribution in the manifest and delivery credits. A provider's general free-stock page is not a substitute for checking the individual asset licence. See [the Imported VFX-first policy](FF_V2_IMPORTED_VFX_POLICY.md).
